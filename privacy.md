# Privacy policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

## Purpose and audience

Coal Drive Automation is a private-use file-management tool. It is operated for an authorized account owner, not offered as a public service. This policy describes the tool's intended use of Google Drive access and the public documentation website.

## Google Drive access

The tool requests Google Drive authorization to carry out file-management tasks directed by the account owner. Those tasks may require reading file names and metadata, downloading file contents, uploading files, creating folders, and moving or deleting specifically selected items. The broad Drive authorization permits these operations; it is not an instruction to perform them without the owner's direction.

The tool uses OAuth rather than collecting the account's Google password. OAuth credentials are stored in access-restricted local configuration. They are not published on this website or in its source repository.

## Use and disclosure

Drive data is used to perform the owner's requested tasks. Local automation may work with an AI assistant. When the owner requests an AI-assisted task involving file contents, relevant information may be processed by the configured AI service. File-transfer tasks do not inherently require sending file contents to an AI model.

The tool does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. It does not use Google user data to develop or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Data is not intentionally published or shared with other people unless the owner directs that action. The privacy terms of Google and any services the owner chooses also apply to their processing.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must comply with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements.

## Storage and retention

Downloaded files, generated outputs and operational records may remain in the owner's local files, private Drive folders or backups until the owner removes them. The tool does not promise automatic deletion after a fixed period. Public documentation contains no Drive files, account credentials or private operational records.

## Account control

The owner can revoke the tool's Google access at any time using [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete copies already downloaded, generated outputs or backups. Those can be removed separately by the owner.

## This website

The documentation website does not contain advertising, application analytics, sign-in forms or a mechanism for uploading files. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information needed to serve the site under its own privacy policy. The site itself has no access to the owner's Drive account.

## Contact and changes

The tool's operator handles privacy requests through the existing private support channel used for the installation. Do not submit personal information or secrets through public repository issues. This page will be updated if the tool's data handling changes.
